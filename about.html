About YMR ShipChandlers

Discover our commitment to quality, our journey in the maritime industry, and why we are the preferred choice for vessel supplies in India.

Our Story

A Legacy of Reliability

YMR ShipChandlers provides essential supplies and services to ships and seafarers, with deep expertise in the maritime industry.

Our foundation is built on the principles of quality, speed, and uncompromising service. We understand the critical nature of maritime operations and the importance of timely, high-quality supplies for vessel performance and crew welfare.

Based in Brahmapur, Odisha, we have expanded our reach to cover all major Indian ports, providing a seamless supply chain for international shipping lines.

Our Mission

To deliver excellence in shipping services, providing comprehensive maritime solutions that ensure vessel operations run smoothly and efficiently across all major Indian ports.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and reliable ship chandler in India, known for quality products, timely delivery, and exceptional customer service in the maritime industry.
